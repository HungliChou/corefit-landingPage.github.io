6. AI Features and Data Processing

The App's AI features, including AI training plan generation and smart weight suggestions, are technically supported by OpenAI. When you use AI features:

6.1 Data Sent to AI

We transmit requests and receive responses through the OpenAI Responses API using HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections. To generate personalized training suggestions, the following data may be sent to OpenAI's API:

  • your fitness goals and preferences
  • body data (height, weight, body fat percentage)
  • exercise experience level
  • training history (exercises, weights, reps, etc.)
  • text you enter in AI conversations

6.2 AI Data Processing Principles

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Under OpenAI commercial API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train OpenAI models. We have not enabled any additional data sharing or model training options.
  • We do not send your email address or account password in AI requests.
  • OpenAI may retain API request and response data within the scope allowed by its policies for abuse monitoring, security, and service provision. According to OpenAI's current public explanation, the Responses API may store application state by default for about 30 days. Actual retention periods, controls, and exceptions depend on OpenAI's then-effective policies.

Coach Invitations and Your Workout Records

Coaches on the Coach plan can send you an invitation using your username. A coach cannot view your workout records without your knowledge or consent.

  • Before you accept: the coach can see only your username and profile photo. The coach cannot see your workout records, schedules, or other personal data, and cannot assign workouts to you. You may decline the invitation or simply ignore it.
  • After you accept: the coach can view all workouts you schedule yourself and your workout records (past and upcoming, including notes you write), and can assign workouts to you. After you accept, the coach can also see your display name. If the coach's Coach plan expires, the coach can still view your workouts and workout records (including those you add after the expiry) for as long as the relationship continues, but cannot invite new students.
  • Coach notes, teams, and analytics: coaches can write private notes about their students (visible only to that coach) and can organize students into teams. When you finish a workout, you may optionally rate its overall perceived exertion (RPE, 1–10); the rating is visible only to you and your coach. While the coach–student relationship lasts, the system prepares summaries of your training (including workouts you log yourself, updated daily while the coach's Coach plan is active) for your coach's analytics; your coach sees the resulting statistics.
  • You can end the relationship at any time: you can end the relationship with your coach at any time under Social › Friends › My Coaches in the App, without the coach's involvement and with no waiting period. After that, the coach can no longer view your workout records or workout content through the Service and can no longer assign workouts to you; workouts you have already received remain in your account. For workouts the coach assigned to you, the assignment record, the completion status of each session, and completion dates are retained (shown as a former student, with your username at the time the relationship ended); training you completed before the relationship ended is counted in aggregate in the coach's team statistics. The coach's private notes about you, your team grouping, and your daily training summaries are deleted when the relationship ends. A coach can also remove a student at any time, with the same effect as you ending the relationship.
  • After the relationship ends or you are removed, the coach may send you an invitation again; no relationship is formed unless you accept again.
  • If you are a coach sending invitations: once you send an invitation, your username and profile photo are shown to the invitee (on their invitation card; the invitation push notification also shows your username), even if they do not accept. After they accept, they can also see your display name.

9. Data Storage and Protection

9.1 Storage Location

  • Cloud storage: your account data, profile, workout records, and other User Content are stored on Appwrite Cloud servers. (Note: Appwrite Cloud servers may be located outside your country or region.)
  • Local storage: to provide offline use, some data is cached on your device.

9.2 Security Measures

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for network transmission
  • encrypted password storage, not plaintext
  • Appwrite access controls so users can access only their own data, except for coaches you have consented to
  • privacy controls for profile fields so you can manage visibility

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously work to protect your information.

9.3 Data Retention

  • Account data and related content are retained while your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify account-related personal data within 30 days, unless continued retention is required by law or legitimate business purposes such as transaction records, refunds, taxes, dispute handling, fraud prevention, or security audits; copies of content you have shared with others (including workouts a coach assigned to you and the records you sent back on them) remain in their accounts and are not deleted, as described in Section 11.2.
  • Subscription, one-time purchase, refund, point, and seat transaction records are handled according to Apple/Google/RevenueCat policies and our retention needs for transactions, entitlements, and disputes.

11. Account and Data Deletion

11.1 How to Delete Your Account

You may request deletion of your CoreFit account through:

  • In App: Me > Settings > Delete Account, then follow the prompts to complete verification and deletion.
  • Email: send an account deletion request to support@corefit-ai.com.

11.2 What Happens After Account Deletion

After your account is deleted:

  • Your profile, workout records, custom exercises, training plans, workout templates, profile images, and similar data will be deleted or de-identified.
  • To preserve the integrity of other users' data and experience, records created jointly with other users (friend relationships, coach–student relationships, and workouts or plans you shared) will not be removed from those users' accounts. Instead, your account will be marked as "Deleted Account" in such records, and your identifiable information (display name, username, and profile photo) will be removed or de-identified from them.
  • Coach invitations that are pending or were declined will be deleted outright and will not remain in the other user's account.
  • If you are a student, workouts a coach assigned to you, including the workout records you sent back on them (such as completion status, weights, reps, and RPE), are shared content: their copies remain in the coach's account and will not be deleted when you delete your account (if you ended the coach–student relationship before deleting your account, the coach can no longer view them through the Service; see "Coach Invitations and Your Workout Records" in Section 8). In addition, for workouts a coach assigned to a team, the assignment records (which workouts were assigned, and each session's completion status and completion date) will remain in the coach's team statistics in de-identified form. For workouts assigned to you individually, the assignment records will be deleted, as will the coach's private notes about you, your team grouping, and training summaries prepared for the coach's analytics; your training content will no longer be counted in the coach's team statistics.
  • If you are a coach, the teams, assignment records, student notes, and related statistics you created will also be deleted.
  • In-app notification data will be deleted or disassociated.
  • Content you have shared with others (such as workouts and plans shared with friends, and the workout records you sent back on workouts a coach assigned to you) remains as copies in their accounts and will not be deleted when you delete your account.
  • Your subscription will not be cancelled automatically. Please cancel your subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play Store before deleting your account, otherwise charges may continue.
  • Except for the records and copies retained as described above, data will be removed from our servers within 30 days after account deletion.
  • Some data may be retained longer due to legal requirements or legitimate business needs, such as transaction records.
