This Policy applies to our collection and processing of personal data when you download and use CoreFit through Apple App Store, Google Play, or other authorized channels. This Policy does not apply to the privacy practices of third-party websites or services, even if linked from the App.

3.2 Data from Third-Party Sign-In

If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, we receive your profile name and email address. We do not receive your third-party account password.

3.3 Subscription and Payment Data

When you purchase subscriptions, points, or coach seat packs, transactions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not directly collect your credit card number or other financial account information. Payment-related data obtained or processed through RevenueCat and platform transaction mechanisms includes anonymous or platform user identifiers, product IDs, transaction IDs, subscription status, plan type (Pro/Coach), subscription period, renewal status, trial status, purchase history, and point/seat entitlement status.

3.4 Data We Do Not Collect

For clarity, the App does not collect:

  • precise location (GPS) or coarse location data
  • advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • contacts or phone numbers
  • SMS or call history
  • continuous camera or microphone access (only one-time photo library or camera access when you choose to upload a profile photo)
  • health platform data (HealthKit, Google Fit)
  • browsing history or usage data from other apps
  • we do not actively use cookies or similar technologies for cross-site tracking or advertising tracking

Third-party services or platforms may process IP address, device information, system version, transaction identifiers, cookies, WebView storage data, or other necessary technical data for transaction verification, security, fraud prevention, abuse detection, video playback, or service maintenance under their own privacy policies.

6. AI Features and Data Processing

The App's AI features, including AI training plan generation and smart weight suggestions, are technically supported by OpenAI. When you use AI features:

6.1 Data Sent to AI

We transmit requests and receive responses through the OpenAI Responses API using HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections. To generate personalized training suggestions, the following data may be sent to OpenAI's API:

  • your fitness goals and preferences
  • body data (height, weight, body fat percentage)
  • exercise experience level
  • training history (exercises, weights, reps, etc.)
  • text you enter in AI conversations

6.2 AI Data Processing Principles

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Under OpenAI commercial API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train OpenAI models. We have not enabled any additional data sharing or model training options.
  • We do not send your email address or account password in AI requests.
  • OpenAI may retain API request and response data within the scope allowed by its policies for abuse monitoring, security, and service provision. According to OpenAI's current public explanation, the Responses API may store application state by default for about 30 days. Actual retention periods, controls, and exceptions depend on OpenAI's then-effective policies.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Except for the third-party services described above, we share personal data only in the following situations:

  1. With your consent: when you actively choose to share workout schedules or plans with friends/students.
  2. Social features: your username and profile photo may be visible to friends/students; other profile fields are visible according to your privacy settings.
  3. Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government authority.
  4. Rights protection: when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or the public.
  5. Business transactions: if a merger, acquisition, or asset sale occurs, your data may be transferred with the business. We will notify you before transfer in such cases.

We do not sell your personal data.

9. Data Storage and Protection

9.1 Storage Location

  • Cloud storage: your account data, profile, workout records, and other User Content are stored on Appwrite Cloud servers. (Note: Appwrite Cloud servers may be located outside your country or region.)
  • Local storage: to provide offline use, some data is cached on your device.

9.2 Security Measures

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for network transmission
  • encrypted password storage, not plaintext
  • Appwrite access controls so users can access only their own data
  • privacy controls for profile fields so you can manage visibility

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously work to protect your information.

9.3 Data Retention

  • Account data and related content are retained while your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify account-related personal data within 30 days, unless continued retention is required by law or legitimate business purposes such as transaction records, refunds, taxes, dispute handling, fraud prevention, or security audits.
  • Subscription, one-time purchase, refund, point, and seat transaction records are handled according to Apple/Google/RevenueCat policies and our retention needs for transactions, entitlements, and disputes.

10. Your Rights

Under applicable laws, including Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  1. Inquiry and access: ask whether we hold your personal data and request access.
  2. Correction and supplementation: request correction or supplementation of inaccurate or incomplete data. You can also directly edit most profile data in the App.
  3. Deletion: request deletion of your personal data (see Section 11).
  4. Stop collection, processing, or use: request that we stop collecting, processing, or using your data in certain circumstances.
  5. Data portability: request data in a structured, machine-readable format where technically feasible.

To exercise these rights, contact us using Section 15. We will respond within a reasonable period after receiving your request, not exceeding 30 days.

11. Account and Data Deletion

11.1 How to Delete Your Account

You may request deletion of your CoreFit account through:

  • In App: Me > Settings > Delete Account, then follow the prompts to complete verification and deletion.
  • Email: send an account deletion request to support@corefit-ai.com.

11.2 What Happens After Account Deletion

After your account is deleted:

  • Your profile, workout records, custom exercises, training plans, workout templates, profile images, and similar data will be deleted or de-identified.
  • Your friend/student relationships, sharing data directly associated with your account, and in-app notification data will be deleted or disassociated.
  • Training content already shared with others may continue to exist as copies in their accounts.
  • Your subscription will not be cancelled automatically. Please cancel your subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play Store before deleting your account, otherwise charges may continue.
  • After account deletion, data will be removed from our servers within 30 days.
  • Some data may be retained longer due to legal requirements or legitimate business needs, such as transaction records.

12. Minors' Privacy

The App is content-rated 4+ on Apple App Store and Google Play, but the Service is not primarily targeted at children under 13.

Children under 13: we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will delete it as soon as practicable and close the account. If you are a parent or legal guardian and wish to consent to a child under 13 using the Service, please first contact us by email at support@corefit-ai.com for identity and consent verification.

Minors aged 13 or older but under the legal age of majority: their legal representative should read and agree to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service before they use the Service.

If you are the legal representative of a minor user and wish to inquire, correct, stop use of, or delete that minor user's personal data, please contact us using Section 15.
